Audience & Tasks

Audience

Parents of prospective students and students considering Duke Kunshan University may use graduate outcomes as an indicator of academic strength and future opportunities.

Tasks

The report communicates and supports recruitment: it highlights graduate outcomes, shows postgraduate destinations, and presents DKU as an attractive option for prospective students and families.

Critique

Strengths

  • Clear outcome emphasis. Large numbers make major achievements immediately visible.
  • Concrete evidence. Selected schools and programs make outcomes feel specific rather than abstract.
  • Recognizable structure. Card-based sections separate broad outcome topics at a glance.

Weaknesses

  • Visual overload. Cards, numbers, logos, and text compete for attention.
  • Limited comparison. Dense school lists make it difficult to compare destinations or spot patterns.
  • Little user control. The static format does not let viewers filter information for the group or destination they care about.

Redesign Strategy

The redesign responds to those weaknesses through three coordinated changes.

  1. Interactive views. Group controls let viewers focus on one student population at a time, reducing visual overload and supporting purposeful exploration.
  2. Geographic exploration. The map shows how postgraduate destinations are distributed, making geographic patterns easier to recognize and destinations easier to browse.
  3. Outcome visibility. A dedicated evidence band keeps key reported achievements visible without combining incompatible measures in one chart.
