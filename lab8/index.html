Lab 8: Web Text Data and Visualization: Exploring a Large Bulletin

Bulletin and Corpus Description

The corpus uses the Duke Kunshan University Undergraduate Bulletin, 2021–22 edition. The PDF was provided with this lab as data/lab8_bulletin.pdf and accessed on September 26, 2026. It was divided into short paragraphs or policy blocks while preserving chapter, section, subsection, page, and passage text.

Semantic Findings

  1. The largest semantic topics are Politics, Culture and Public Policy (279 passages), Quantitative, Data and Computing (210), and Academic Credits and Graduation (180).
  2. All eight topics appear across multiple formal sections, showing that the bulletin’s formal hierarchy and its semantic organization are not identical.
  3. Course Descriptions contains six topics, while Student Research Opportunities contains four, making them the most semantically diverse formal sections.
  4. A substring search for credit returns 776 passages across seven semantic topics, whereas graduation appears in four topics and registration in three.

Design Description

Each cleaned paragraph or short policy block was treated as one document. The corpus contains 1,265 passages after duplicate removal and removal of course-table fragments, with chapter, section, subsection, page, and text retained for every record. I generated normalized 384-dimensional semantic embeddings with the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 SentenceTransformer model. UMAP projected these vectors to two dimensions using cosine distance, 15 neighbors, a minimum distance of 0.15, and random state 401. The x and y positions are therefore used only as an approximate semantic neighborhood, not as independent variables. I applied KMeans with eight clusters to the original embedding vectors, inspected representative passages and TF-IDF terms, and named the resulting topics. The semantic map uses position for the projection, color for topic, and radius for passage length. Search and the two filters reduce visual prominence for nonmatching passages, while click opens the original structural metadata and five nearest passages calculated by cosine distance in the original embedding space. Zoom and pan allow local inspection. The matrix summarizes the relation between formal sections and semantic topics with passage counts; its tooltip exposes the exact section, topic, and count. It displays zero-count combinations in the lightest color. Clicking a matrix cell highlights the corresponding passages in the map, and clicking a map point highlights its matrix cell. Together, the two views connect where a passage occurs in the bulletin with what it discusses.
