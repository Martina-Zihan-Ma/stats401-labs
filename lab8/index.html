Lab 8: Web Text Data and Visualization: Exploring a Large Bulletin

Bulletin and Corpus Description

The corpus uses the Duke Kunshan University Undergraduate Bulletin, 2021–22 edition. The PDF was provided with this lab as data/lab8_bulletin.pdf and accessed on September 26, 2026. It was divided into short paragraphs or policy blocks while preserving chapter, section, subsection, page, and passage text.

Semantic Findings

  1. The largest semantic topics are China, Language and Global Education (319 passages), Arts, Media and Digital Practice (228), and Politics, Culture and Public Policy (225).
  2. All eight topics appear across multiple formal sections, showing that the bulletin’s formal hierarchy and its semantic organization are not identical.
  3. Course Descriptions contains all eight topics; the major overview contains seven, while Career Services and Degree Requirements contain five each.
  4. A substring search for credit returns 895 passages across all eight topics, whereas graduation appears in three topics and registration in three.

Design Description

Each cleaned paragraph or short policy block was treated as one document. I used the bulletin’s bold 16-, 14-, and 12-point PDF hierarchy for chapter, section, and subsection detection rather than title-case guesses; this prevents table labels, list continuations, and contact addresses from becoming sections. The corpus contains 1,616 passages after deduplication and malformed-signature removal, with chapter, section, subsection, page, and text retained. I generated normalized 384-dimensional embeddings with all-MiniLM-L6-v2. UMAP projected them to two dimensions with cosine distance, 15 neighbors, minimum distance .15, and random state 401, so axes are only an approximate neighborhood. I applied eight-cluster KMeans to the original embeddings, inspected representative passages and TF-IDF terms, and named the topics. Position encodes the projection, color topic, and radius passage length. Search and filters dim nonmatches; clicking exposes structural metadata and five cosine-nearest passages. Zoom and pan support close inspection. The matrix gives section-by-topic counts, including zeroes in the lightest color, and its tooltip gives the exact cell values. Matrix-cell clicks highlight matching map points, and map-point clicks highlight their matrix cell, linking bulletin location with semantic content.
